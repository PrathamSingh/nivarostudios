Buy me a coffee

Pay whatever you like — or help for nothing, which matters just as much.

Every app here is free, has no adverts in the sideload builds, collects nothing about you and works without an account. That is a deliberate choice, and it means none of them earn anything on their own.

If one of them has saved you a wrong platform, a missed last train or a fare you did not expect, you are welcome to put something in the tin. There is no paywall behind this, nothing unlocks, and nothing changes if you do not.

Where it actually goes. A Google Play developer account is a one-off $25. The domain is about $12 a year. Everything else — the maps, the transit data, the hosting — is free tier or open data, and the rest is time. Roughly ₹300 covers a month of running costs across all the apps.

Give something

Pick any amount on the next page. Most people give between ₹100 and ₹300. Payment is handled entirely by Razorpay — UPI, cards, net banking or wallets — on their own secure page.

Or help for free

Honestly, these are worth more than the money, and they cost you nothing:

  • Rate the app. Store ranking is driven by ratings far more than by anything a developer can write in a listing.
  • Report something wrong. A misnamed station or a fare that does not match the gate is the most valuable message I can get — support@nivarostudios.com.
  • Tell one person who takes that metro.

Being straight with you

  • This is support for an independent developer, not a charity donation. Nivaro Studios is a sole proprietorship in Delhi, India. It is not a registered non-profit, your payment is not tax-deductible, and I am not collecting on anyone else’s behalf.
  • Nothing is unlocked by paying. There is no supporter tier, no removed advert, no hidden feature. Every app is the same app for everyone.
  • There are no refunds to chase because there is nothing owed — but if you paid by mistake, write to me and I will send it straight back, no questions.
  • Please do not give money you need. Rating the app is genuinely more useful than ₹100 you would rather keep.

Your privacy

This page sets no cookies, loads no scripts and counts no visitors. When you tap through, Razorpay handles the payment under their own privacy policy and tells me only the amount and the name you choose to give. I never see your card number, your UPI PIN, or your bank details — those never touch any server of mine, because there is no server of mine.
