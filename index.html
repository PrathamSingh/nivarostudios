How we build

Three rules we do not bend.

They decide what goes into an app, and more often what stays out of it.

Your data stays put

What you type, record or save lives in the app's private storage on your phone. We have no server holding it and no copy to hand over.

No account to open

Install it and it works. Where signing in unlocks something extra, it is optional and the rest of the app carries on without it.

The fewest permissions possible

Most of our apps ask for none at all. Every permission we do not request is one less thing you have to trust us with.

Something broken? Tell us.

Questions, bug reports or anything about your data — we read every message. If you are writing about a specific app, telling us which one and which phone you are on helps us fix things faster.
