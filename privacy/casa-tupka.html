Casa Tupka Master — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Contact: support@nivarostudios.com

Casa Tupka Master is a merge puzzle game: you combine two matching relics to make the next one up, and fill the temple's orders. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version is that there is no information — the game is a single-player puzzle that runs entirely on your phone.

Summary

  • The game asks for no runtime permissions at all — no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone, no storage.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. The game never asks who you are.
  • Casa Tupka Master has no server and makes no network requests. It works identically in airplane mode.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in this version.

What is stored, and where

The game writes exactly two things to your device's own private app storage:

  • Your saved game — the board, your relics, your energy, which orders you have filled and how far you have progressed.
  • Your settings — whether sound and vibration are on, and your theme preference.

Neither contains anything personal. Neither leaves the device. There is no copy anywhere else, including with us. Uninstalling the game deletes both.

Advertising

The current version of Casa Tupka Master contains no advertising. No advertising SDK is included in the build, so no ad network receives your device identifier or anything else.

A future version may offer an optional rewarded video — the kind you choose to watch in exchange for something in the game, never one that interrupts you. If that ships, this policy will be updated before that version is published, the advertising partner will be named here, and the store listing's data disclosure will be updated with it. We will not add advertising to a published version without changing this page first.

Analytics and crash reporting

There are none. We do not know how many levels you have played, how long you play, or whether the game has ever crashed for you. If something goes wrong, the only way we learn about it is if you write to us.

Sound and vibration

The game plays its own bundled sound effects and uses your phone's vibration motor for feedback. Playing a sound does not require the microphone permission and the game does not have it — nothing is ever recorded or listened to. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Links out of the game

A few screens offer links to this website, to our support email address and to the game's store listing. They open only when you tap them, in your own browser or mail app. Until you tap one, the game makes no outbound connection of any kind.

Children

Casa Tupka Master is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in a children's category. Because the game collects nothing from anyone, there is nothing held about a user of any age, and no profile is built for advertising or any other purpose.

Your rights and deletions

Everything lives on your own device, so you are in direct control:

  • Reset your progress from the game's Settings screen.
  • Clearing the app's data in Android Settings erases the save and the settings.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything.

We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and no request to make. If you have a question, write to support@nivarostudios.com.

Grievance redressal

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 requires a published point of contact for privacy questions and complaints. For every Nivaro Studios app that is:

Grievance Officer, Nivaro Studios
support@nivarostudios.com
Delhi, India

We acknowledge a grievance within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

Withdrawing consent

There is no consent to withdraw, because nothing is collected to consent to. If you would like the game to stop holding anything at all, clear its data in Android Settings or uninstall it — that removes everything, immediately and permanently, with no request to us needed.

If there were ever a breach

Your save stays on your phone, so there is no server of ours holding it and no database of ours to breach. In the unlikely event of an incident that nonetheless affected you, we would publish it on this page and notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and any change that affects what the game collects will ship alongside the app update that introduces it.
